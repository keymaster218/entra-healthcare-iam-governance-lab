# Northstar Health Services

## Organization Overview

Northstar Health Services is a fictional regional healthcare organization providing outpatient care, administrative services, and clinical support operations.

The organization is modernizing its identity infrastructure using Microsoft Entra ID to improve security, access governance, and audit readiness.

---

## Business Drivers

Northstar Health Services must:

- Protect sensitive healthcare information.
- Enforce least-privilege access.
- Reduce standing administrative privileges.
- Improve audit readiness.
- Support workforce mobility.
- Strengthen identity governance.

---

## Departments

- Health Information Management
- Revenue Cycle
- Clinical Application Support
- Information Technology
- Security Operations
- Cloud Operations
- Compliance

---

## Security Objectives

- Require multi-factor authentication.
- Implement role-based access control.
- Perform quarterly access reviews.
- Establish privileged access controls.
- Maintain audit evidence.
- Support HIPAA-aligned access management.

---

## Identity Challenges

The organization currently experiences:

- Excessive administrative privileges.
- Inconsistent role assignments.
- Limited access review processes.
- Shared administrative accounts.
- Manual onboarding and offboarding procedures.

This project demonstrates how identity and access management controls can address these challenges.
